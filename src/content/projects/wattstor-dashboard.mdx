Podium users couldn't access key metrics, like ROI, battery utilisation, renewable consumption, without downloading raw CSVs and running manual calculations. This forced Wattstor staff to field constant reporting requests that should have been self-serve, whilst partners and customers waited for answers they needed for their own reporting."
